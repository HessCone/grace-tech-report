Global corporate AI investment reached $581.7 billion in 2025, up 130% from 2024, and private investment reached $344.7 billion, up 127.5%, according to the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI’s 2026 AI Index (published April 13, 2026). The same report estimated the value of generative AI tools to U.S. consumers at $172 billion a year by early 2026. AI data-center power capacity rose to 29.6 GW. On Terminal-Bench, the success rate of agents handling real-world tasks rose from 20% in 2025 to 77.3%.

NVIDIA’s latest reported quarter, the third quarter of fiscal 2027 ended October 25, 2026, brought in $115.8 billion, up 20% from the prior quarter and 103% from a year earlier. The company said it had begun shipping Vera Rubin NVL72 racks and guided fourth-quarter revenue to $130.0 billion, plus or minus 3%, with no assumed data-center compute revenue from China.

Electric cars

IEA reported totals IEA 2026 forecast

Global electric car sales (battery-electric and plug-in hybrid), in millions. 2020–2025 are the IEA’s published round totals (“about 3 million,” “6.6 million,” “more than 10 million,” “nearly 14 million,” “exceeded 17 million,” “exceeded 20 million”). 2026 is the IEA’s forecast of 23 million, not a final count.

  • 2025 sales share
    25%
    One in four new cars sold worldwide was electric. Sales exceeded 20 million, up 20% from 2024. IEA Global EV Outlook 2026.
  • 2026 sales share outlook
    29%
    Raised one point from the May outlook after a strong second quarter. IEA, July 30, 2026.
  • China, 2025
    ~55%
    Share of China’s car sales that were electric. Heading toward almost 60% in 2026. IEA.
  • United States, 2025
    <10%
    Sales stayed relatively stable at just under 10% of the U.S. car market. IEA.

The International Energy Agency’s Global EV Outlook 2026, published in May, said electric car sales grew 20% in 2025 to exceed 20 million, or one-quarter of all new cars. Europe was the fastest-growing major market, up more than 30% to a 28% sales share. In a July 30, 2026 update, the IEA raised its 2026 worldwide share forecast to 29%, with full-year sales still expected at 23 million. First-quarter 2026 global sales were about 3.9 million, 8% lower than a year earlier, mainly because of weaker sales in China and the United States after policy changes. Sales then rebounded in the second quarter.

The two figures cover different time windows, because Waymo reports quarterly and Tesla reports weekly.

SpaceX is still the world’s busiest launch operator, but 2026 has been a slower year than 2025. Flight Atlas records 130 SpaceX orbital launches through November 26, 2026 UTC, after 170 in all of 2025 and 138 in 2024. The rest of the world has flown 147 attempts this year. Data & sources

What this is

A static briefing of public, sourced figures as of November 25, 2026, Central Time. It is not a live dashboard. Where a source gives a range or a rounded total (“exceeded 20 million,” “just over 1 billion”), that wording is kept. Forecasts are labeled as forecasts.

What it is not

This page does not invent or interpolate missing numbers. Trackers can disagree by a few launches or miles. Robotaxi ride and mile figures are only as recent as each company has disclosed.
